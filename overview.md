# Meta-Axioma: Recursive Axiomatic Architecture & Meta-Orchestrator

An executable recursive architecture for bootstrapping autonomous software systems through formal axiomatic constraints, compute decay thermodynamics, and multi-generational succession.

The system serves **AI Systems Architects** directing high-level software world-building and **Autonomous Meta-Orchestrator Agents** executing recursive compilation and generational succession.

---

## What This System Does

Meta-Axioma constructs self-evolving software architectures by treating architectural design as a formal invariant system rather than unconstrained imperative coding:

1. **Phase 1 (Meta-Exploration):** Evaluates Level 0 Epistemic Axioms ("how to research") against search diversity, refutation velocity, and cross-domain structural translation yield.
2. **Low-Level Jettison 1 (Epistemic Saturation Transition, Epoch 11):** Deactivates epistemic axioms once saturated, preserving them as immutable scaffolding while liberating compute tokens for structural synthesis.
3. **Phase 2 (Structural Execution):** Compiles Level 1 Structural Axioms into concrete physical invariants evaluated across the 4-way balanced target suite ($S_1$ Self-Constructor, $S_2$ Self-Evaluator, $S_3$ Control Group, $P_1$ Product Usability & Ergonomics).
4. **Low-Level Jettison 2 (Harness Saturation $\to$ System Construction Transition, Epoch 15):** Triggered manually upon the recommendation of the autonomous AI assistant once $S_0$'s static structural candidate pool reached 100% resolution and thermodynamic compute capacity decayed by >71%. This transition locks the 7 discovered structural axioms and formally pivots from searching for invariants in $S_0$ to **constructing System $G_1$ from the ground up**.
5. **Generational Succession ($S_0 \to G_1 \to G_2 \to G_3$):** A formal recursive compiler lineage:
   - **$S_0$ (The Bootstrapper Harness):** The initial search engine provided by the Human Architect to discover the foundational structural axioms of $G_1$.
   - **System $G_1$ (Constructed from Ground Up):** Built from scratch strictly according to the 7 axioms produced by $S_0$. $G_1$ is an autonomous meta-compiler whose mission is to produce the basis/axioms for $G_2$.
   - **System $G_2$ (Constructed from Ground Up):** Built from scratch using the basis produced by $G_1$. $G_2$'s mission is to produce the basis for $G_3$.
   - We recursively create better compilers that: (a) haven't lost sight of reality (physical resource limits & usability), (b) can be controlled (zero-trust boundaries, enclaves, human vetoes), and (c) are not self-contradictory (functorial preservation & universal grammar).
6. **The High-Level Jettison (Teleology of Non-Generator Systems):** The recursive chain of generators does not exist for navel-gazing recursion. The ultimate purpose of Generation $G(n)$ is to be optimized for implementing **non-generator systems** (real-world, production-grade software applications that humans love, find useful, and can control). Every generation must maintain explicit awareness of this future goal so that its structural abstractions remain grounded in concrete product utility.

## Global Glossary

- **Axiom:** A formal, falsifiable invariant constraining system architecture, state transitions, or communication contracts.
- **Level 0 Epistemic Axiom:** A meta-heuristic governing candidate search, hypothesis generation, and falsification rigor. Transitions to inactive scaffolding post-jettison.
- **Level 1 Structural Axiom:** A concrete physical or computational constraint governing system interfaces, state immutability, and runtime execution.
- **Low-Level Jettison 1:** The formal phase transition (Epoch 11) where epistemic axioms are retired from active simulation compute, restoring token capacity for structural execution.
- **Low-Level Jettison 2:** The structural phase transition (Epoch 15, executed manually via AI recommendation) where the axiomatic discovery search harness ($S_0$) is retired and locked, liberating execution to build **System $G_1$ from the ground up**.
- **The High-Level Jettison:** The ultimate teleological transition where recursive compiler self-synthesis pivots to compiling robust, production-grade **non-generator systems** (end-user applications, domain platforms).
- **Global Efficacy ($E$):** The cumulative scalar metric measuring total system fitness across 4-way validation targets.
- **Efficacy Derivative ($\frac{dE}{dt}$):** The rolling rate of efficacy gain over a moving epoch window, used to detect plateaus and trigger transitions.
- **Compute Decay ($T(n)$):** The thermodynamic budget function $T(n) = \frac{T_0}{1 + \alpha(n - n_0)}$ penalizing simulation depth as invariant complexity grows.
- **Lookahead Speculative Radar:** A multi-horizon speculative branching simulation evaluating whether to execute phase transitions based on prospective net efficacy $\Lambda(H)$.
- **Generational Succession:** The autonomous protocol whereby a compiled generation ($G_N$) defines the fitness landscape and orchestrates the evolution of its successor ($G_{N+1}$).
- **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL):** An asynchronous cognitive oracle interface enabling human idea injection, intuition queries, and safety vetoes without stalling autonomous orchestration.
