Axioma PhenoFlora: Production Temporal-Spatial Ecological Studio

An autonomous, mathematically grounded ecological habitat design studio engineered to resolve urban bloom droughts and specialist pollinator starvation through continuous 52-week Gaussian bloom convolution, dual-trophic energetic balancing (Nectar Joules vs. Pollen Protein), 2D spatial physics with seasonal canopy raymarching, pure-Python First-Order Logic SMT continuity proofs, and functorial bit-reversible state transitions.

The studio serves Ecological Designers and Urban Conservationists crafting resilient pollinator habitats that provide continuous nutritional continuity across critical seasonal transitions.

What This System Does

Axioma PhenoFlora transforms ecological landscape planning from static plant lists into a dynamic, verified cyber-physical system:

1. Continuous 52-Week Gaussian Bloom Convolution: Evaluates flowering timelines across 52 weeks using continuous Gaussian distributions rather than crude monthly buckets, capturing acute seasonal troughs and early-spring/late-autumn nutritional deficits.

2. Dual-Trophic Energetic Curves: Simultaneously simulates two distinct nutritional currencies:

  • Nectar Caloric Value ($E_{\text{nectar}}$, Joules): Fuels adult pollinator flight metabolic requirements.
  • Pollen Protein Rating ($P_{\text{pollen}}$, Grams): Essential for larval brood development and queen reproduction.

3. 2D Spatial Physics & Seasonal Canopy Raymarching: Models physical plant placement, mature spread collision detection, and dynamic solar shadow projections across calendar weeks based on solar elevation, azimuth, and plant mature heights.

4. Oligolectic Dependency & Specialization Engine: Enforces mutualistic host constraints for specialist solitary bees (e.g., *Colletes*, *Andrena*, *Osmia*) with obligate floral genera dependencies.

5. Pure-Python FOL/SMT Continuity Prover: Mathematically verifies that no pollinator guild experiences a nutritional gap exceeding guild starvation tolerances, producing deterministic satisfaction or counter-proof gap certificates.

6. Pareto Remediation Optimizer: Synthesizes ranked plant recommendations that simultaneously maximize gap coverage, specialist bee survival, and spatial fit without introducing canopy crowding.

7. Functorial Bit-Reversible State Transitions ($f \circ f^{-1} = \text{id}$): Maintains a deterministic tape of algebraic morphisms enabling lossless forward/backward time-travel across scenario branches.

Global Glossary

  • Trophic Curve: A continuous 52-week energetic vector representing total floral reward (nectar or pollen) available in the habitat per week.
  • Bloom Drought: A contiguous time interval ($[w_{\text{start}}, w_{\text{end}}]$) during which available nectar or pollen falls below the minimum survival threshold of active pollinator guilds.
  • Oligolectic Dependency: A strict ecological obligate relationship where a specialist pollinator requires floral rewards from specific plant genera for larval survival.
  • Canopy Shadow Projection: A polygon cast by a mature plant based on seasonal solar angles, reducing photosynthetic active radiation on lower understory plantings.
  • Remediation Candidate: A plant species evaluated and ranked by the multi-objective Pareto solver to fill identified bloom deficits while respecting bed microclimate constraints.
  • Functorial Morphism: A state transition equipped with a mathematically verified inverse function restoring the exact previous state hash.
