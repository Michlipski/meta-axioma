# Generation 4b Sovereign Research Service (`services/research`)

## Overview
The **Sovereign Research Service** (`services/research`) operates as the independent specification and formal verification enclave of Generation 4b. Under the Decoupled Research Sovereignty Invariant (`[L1-G4B-001]`) and the Zero-Code Boundary (`[L1-G4B-002]`), the Research Service is physically barred from executing or writing production code. Its sole permitted deliverables are formal `ResearchDeliverableBundle` specifications, domain models, statecharts, and protocol schemas.

1. **Waterloo Tier 1 Situation of Concern (`[L1-G3B-000]`):** Formulates formal Macro/Meso/Micro breakdowns (actors, frictions, constraints, unmet needs) for all application targets.
2. **Formal Domain Modeling (`ContractIR` & `StatechartIR`):** Specifies formal algebraic product/sum domain nouns, category-theoretic composition verbs, and hierarchical XState v5 statecharts.
3. **Z3 SMT Invariant Verification:** Checks candidate state transitions and contract morphisms against system invariants before delivery to the Builder Enclave.
4. **Boundary Isolation:** Bound to dedicated non-loopback IP `192.168.40.10`.
