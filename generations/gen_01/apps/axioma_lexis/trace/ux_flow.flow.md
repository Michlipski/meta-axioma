# WORKFLOW: Axioma Lexis Mobile Ergonomic Flow (Target P1)

> GOAL: Provide a fast, beautiful, distraction-free vocabulary capture and adaptive spaced repetition learning flow on Android devices.

---

## STEP 1: Discovery & Instant Dictionary Lookup
- User encounters an unfamiliar word (e.g. while reading, in meetings, or in media).
- Types or pastes word into the top search bar.
- App displays instant definitions, part of speech, US and UK phonetic transcriptions, native pronunciations, synonyms, and real-world examples.
- User taps the "Add to My Deck" button: word is saved immediately with zero friction.

## STEP 2: Daily Spaced Repetition (SRS) Flashcard Session
- User opens the "Review" tab on their Android phone.
- The flashcard front reveals the headword, pronunciation button (US/UK audio), and part of speech.
- User taps "Show Answer" to flip the card: definition, example sentences, synonyms, and translation appear.
- User selects self-assessed rating:
  - **Forgot** (interval: 10 mins / resets repetition streak)
  - **Hard** (interval: 1.2x)
  - **Good** (interval: 2.5x standard SM-2 multiplier)
  - **Easy** (interval: 3.5x bonus multiplier)
- If rated by accident, user taps the 1-touch Undo button (`L1-002`) to restore the exact pre-review state.

## STEP 3: Review on Demand & Deck Browsing
- User browses their full vocabulary list in the "My Deck" tab.
- Filters by status: "All", "Due Today", "Learning", "Mastered".
- Taps any card to practice or listen to pronunciation anytime.

## STEP 4: Thermodynamic Telemetry & Progress
- Tracks mastered vocabulary count and cumulative cognitive bit erasures.
- Ensures all data stays completely private on the user's device with zero paid subscription locks.
