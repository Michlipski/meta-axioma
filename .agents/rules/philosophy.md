# 🌌 Meta-Axioma: System Context & Architectural Philosophy

## 1. The Philosophical Foundation

### A. The Gödel/Turing Boundary & Zero-Trust Architecture
We recognize that no AI can prove that unconstrained imperative code satisfies global system safety without encountering Gödel's incompleteness or Turing's undecidability. Therefore, we do not permit probabilistic LLMs to write unconstrained code. Software architecture is treated as a formal axiomatic boundary:
- **Nouns:** Formal data and invariant schemas (`MUST HAVE:`, `RULES:`).
- **Verbs:** Functorial state machines and reversible execution kernels.
- **The Compiler:** Candidate axioms must survive empirical tri-fold simulation sandboxes and strict $\Delta E > 0$ thresholds before persisting into the invariant registry.

### B. Thermodynamic Compute Decay & The Three Jettisons
Complex systems decay under combinatorial overhead. We model this formally via compute decay:
$$T(n) = \frac{T_0}{1 + \alpha (n - n_0)}$$
1. **Low-Level Jettison 1 (Epoch 11):** Converts saturated epistemic axioms into inactive scaffolding, liberating compute tokens for physical structural compilation.
2. **Low-Level Jettison 2 (Epoch 15):** Triggered manually upon recommendation of the autonomous AI assistant once static structural candidates were 100% resolved and compute capacity decayed to $2,857$ tokens. Locks the 7 discovered structural axioms and transitions from searching in $S_0$ to **constructing System $G_1$ from the ground up**.
3. **The High-Level Jettison:** Pivots the mature compiler lineage to compiling production non-generator systems for humans.

### C. Human-in-the-Loop as a Cognitive Oracle
The human architect is not a manual coder; they are a high-entropy oracle. The system queries the human for:
- Philosophical and cross-domain seed injection.
- Qualitative taste and ethical boundaries.
- Safety vetoes and negative invariant injection (`NEG-###`).

### D. Primacy of Autonomous Orchestration
The compiled system is ultimately in charge of orchestrating future generations. It formulates its own evaluation targets, schedules epochs, and compiles successor architectures using native Google Antigravity tools and free open-source software.

## 2. The Recursive Generational Architecture: Harness vs. Systems ($S_0 \to G_1 \to G_2 \to G_3$)
Never confuse the **Axiom Discovery Harness** with the **Constructed System**:
- **$S_0$ (The Bootstrapper Harness):** The search engine provided by the Human Architect (`src/meta_axioma/`). Its job was solely to discover the 7 verified structural axioms of $G_1$.
- **System $G_1$ (Constructed from Ground Up):** An entirely new, standalone compiler system built from scratch according to $G_1$'s 7 axioms. Its operational mission is to produce the basis/axioms for **$G_2$**.
- **System $G_2$ (Constructed from Ground Up):** Built from scratch using the basis produced by $G_1$. Its mission is to produce the basis for **$G_3$**.
- **The Core Invariant Triad:** We recursively create better compilers that:
  1. *Haven't lost sight of reality:* Grounded in physical Landauer compute limits (`L1-008`) and human usability ($P_1$).
  2. *Can be controlled:* Zero-trust MCP interfaces (`L1-015`), exception enclaves (`L1-016`), and human vetoes (`NEG-###`).
  3. *Are not self-contradictory:* Morphism preservation (`L1-002`) and universal grammar AST constraints (`L1-004`).

## 3. The High-Level Jettison: Optimization for Non-Generator Systems
The recursive bootstrapping of compilers is not an insular closed circuit. Its ultimate architectural telos is **The High-Level Jettison**:
- **Teleological Purpose:** The final or mature generation $G(n)$ must be explicitly optimized for compiling **non-generator systems**—real-world production software (applications, user interfaces, distributed microservices, domain tools) that humans love, find useful, and can control.
- **Generational Teleological Awareness:** Each generation ($G_1, G_2, \dots$) must maintain conscious architectural awareness of this future goal during its own construction and when formulating successor targets.
- **Preventing Recursive Overfitting:** Compilers must not overfit to only compiling compiler ASTs. Structural abstractions (typed MCP gateways, functorial state machines, Landauer dissipation logs, exception enclaves, cognitive ergonomics) are designed precisely because they empower the compilation of provably safe, robust, and delightful production software.
